Lens Technology

The lens does the work.
The frame gets the credit.

Five specialty lens technologies you'll struggle to find under one roof anywhere else in Chicago. We were early adopters on every one of these, and we'll explain plainly why a particular lens is right for your eyes.

FDA-Authorized

Stellest

Slows myopia progression in children by up to 67%. Worn during waking hours. Game-changing for families with progressive nearsightedness.

Learn more →
Digital Strain

Neurolens

The first prescription lens that addresses eye misalignment behind chronic headaches, neck pain, and screen fatigue.

Learn more →
Migraine Relief

Avulux

Filters specific wavelengths shown to trigger light-sensitive migraine. The most-recommended lens in light-sensitivity protocols.

Learn more →
Color Vision

Enchroma

Engineered lenses that help people with red-green color vision deficiency see color more vividly. Bring tissues for the fitting.
