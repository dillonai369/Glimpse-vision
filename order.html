How it works

Your prescription stays with us.
The logistics happen seamlessly.

We work with industry-trusted shipping and dispensing partners so your supplies arrive fast, fresh, and securely tracked. We verify each order against your file before it goes out.

  • Verified partners. WeWillShip for contact lenses, Myze for eyecare retail, Fullscript for supplements.
  • No new account paperwork. Your existing patient file with Glimpse is what authorizes the reorder.
  • Direct tracking. Order confirmation and tracking come from the partner directly to your inbox.
  • Questions? Call Gold Coast at 312 943 2020 or Hinsdale at 630 325 2020 anytime during store hours.
