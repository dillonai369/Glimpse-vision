Essilor · FDA Authorized Myopia Control

Stellest.

The first FDA-authorized prescription lens proven to slow myopia progression in children — by an average of 71% over two years.*

Book a pediatric myopia consult Our myopia program
The lens

An eyeglass lens designed to slow nearsightedness.

Myopia, often called nearsightedness, is predicted to affect 50% of the world’s population by 2050. The younger a child becomes myopic, the faster it tends to progress — raising lifetime risk of glaucoma, cataracts, and retinal detachment. Stellest is the first prescription eyeglass lens FDA-authorized to do something about it.

Best for: Kids ages 6–12 · Newly myopic patients · Families looking to slow nearsightedness progression

How Stellest works

H.A.L.T. technology, explained.

Although myopia is partly genetic, environmental factors — less time outdoors, more time reading and on devices — play a major role. Stellest uses patented Highly Aspherical Lenslet Target (H.A.L.T.) technology to correct your child’s vision today and slow the elongation of their eye over time.

01.

Crisp central vision

A large single-vision central zone gives your child sharp, comfortable distance vision — classrooms, sports, devices — just like a standard prescription lens.

02.

1,021 invisible lenslets

Around that central zone sit eleven concentric rings of highly aspherical lenslets, creating a volume of signal that gently encourages the eye to slow its elongation.

03.

Worn all day, every day

Stellest works best when worn during all waking hours — the same way your child would wear a standard prescription. No nightly drops, no contact-lens routine.

Clinically proven

71% slower
myopia progression.

On average, over two years of consistent wear, compared with standard single-vision lenses.* Results from a prospective, randomized, double-masked, multicenter U.S. clinical trial in myopic children ages 6–12.

Long-term eye health

Why slowing progression matters.

Higher myopia isn’t just a thicker prescription. The longer the eye elongates, the higher the lifetime risk of sight-threatening conditions — cataracts, glaucoma, and retinal detachment among them. Managing how quickly your child’s eyeball grows during the critical years between 6 and 12 can meaningfully change their adult eye health.

Our myopia control program

Stellest is one of three.

Every child’s eyes — and every family’s lifestyle — are different. Our optometrists fit all three FDA-pathway myopia options and will recommend the right one for your child.

Eyeglass lens

Stellest by Essilor

FDA-authorized myopia control lens worn full-time as standard glasses. Best for kids ages 6–12 who aren’t ready for contacts.
Daily contact

MiSight 1 Day by CooperVision

First FDA-approved soft contact lens for myopia control. Daily disposable — great for active kids and sports.
Specialty multifocal

NaturalVue Multifocal

Off-label specialty daily disposable with neurofocus-optics design — an option when MiSight isn’t the right fit.
