Contact Lenses

See life
without limits.

From everyday daily disposables to specialty designs — multifocal, scleral, RGP, hybrid, and orthokeratology — we fit the full range of contact lenses from the manufacturers we trust most. Whatever your prescription, your eyes, or your lifestyle, there’s a lens that lets you see your way through it.

Lens Types & Fits

Every kind of lens we’re trained to fit.

Daily

Daily Disposables

The easiest, healthiest option for most wearers. Fresh lens every day, no cleaning, ideal for occasional or active wear.

Multifocal

Multifocal Contacts

Distance and near in a single lens, designed for presbyopia and people who'd rather not wear readers over contacts.

Scleral

Scleral Lenses

Large-diameter lenses that vault over the cornea, fit for irregular corneas, severe dry eye, or post-surgical eyes.

RGP

Rigid Gas Permeable

Sharper optics than soft lenses for high prescriptions and irregular corneas. Long-lasting, oxygen-permeable.

Hybrid

Hybrid Lenses

RGP center with a soft skirt — the crispness of rigid lenses with the comfort of soft, ideal for sport.

Ortho-K

Orthokeratology

Overnight reshaping lenses for daytime freedom from glasses or contacts. Strong fit for active kids and teens.
