Anne & Valentin

Color, color, color — French frames that refuse to apologize.

Try Anne & Valentin on at Glimpse All brands
The brand

What makes Anne & Valentin Anne & Valentin.

Anne & Valentin frames are designed by an in-house team in Toulouse who treat eyewear as a form of wearable color theory. Every collection introduces palettes you've genuinely never seen, paired with shapes that flatter without disappearing.

Best for: Color-confident wearers · Architects, artists, designers · Anyone tired of looking polite

Why we carry Anne & Valentin

The details that matter.

Made in France

All frames are designed and manufactured in France, with construction that meets the brand's 4-year warranty.

Personality is the point

If you've been told eyewear should be neutral, Anne & Valentin would like a word.

Sized for every face

Because the brand designs both ends of the size spectrum, we routinely fit clients who struggle elsewhere.

In person

The full Anne & Valentin collection
is in our cases.

Both Glimpse locations carry the active Anne & Valentin lineup. Bring a friend, take photos, try every shape twice. Our opticians have spent decades fitting these frames; their input is part of the price.
