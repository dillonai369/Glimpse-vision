Orgreen.

The brand

What makes Orgreen Orgreen.

Orgreen makes the case that you can do an enormous amount with a single material if you really commit. Their entire lineup is handmade Japanese titanium, finished in over 240 proprietary colors, with shapes that range from minimalist to architectural.

Best for: Minimalists who still want personality · Frequent travelers · High prescriptions

Why we carry Orgreen

The details that matter.

Every brand we carry earns its place. Here’s why Orgreen is in our cases.

01.

Made in Japan, designed in Copenhagen

The factory is the same one that supplies several other top-tier titanium houses.

02.

Color is the differentiator

From soft beige to deep emerald to brushed copper. Every season the palette evolves.

03.

Lightweight for high prescriptions

Titanium with subtle flex for all-day comfort.
