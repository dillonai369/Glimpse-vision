Lindberg.

The brand

What makes Lindberg Lindberg.

Lindberg is the byword for quiet luxury in eyewear. Frames are masterfully assembled in pure titanium and lightweight acetate, every joint engineered to eliminate welds and screws. The result is an object so light you forget you're wearing it — and so well-made you may not need another pair for a decade.

Best for: Lightweight wearers · Minimalists · Anyone with a high prescription who wants thin lenses

Why we carry Lindberg

The details that matter.

Eight collections, infinite combinations

We carry Spirit (rimless), Rim, Acetanium, Thintanium, NOW, Strip, the SUN sunglass collection, and KIDS. Within each, frame size, temple length, color, nose pads, even hinge style can be customized to your face.

Built to last

Lindberg's reputation is built on titanium that flexes without fatigue. We have clients still wearing the same Lindberg frames they bought in the early 2000s.

Bespoke fitting at Glimpse

Our opticians are trained in the full Lindberg fitting protocol — including the precise measurement and adjustment of every component to your face shape.
