Lens Technology

Neurolens

Prescription lenses for digital eye strain and chronic head/neck tension.

Neurolens is the first lens technology designed to address eye misalignment — a condition that affects an estimated two-thirds of the population and is often the hidden driver of headaches, neck pain, dry eye, and persistent screen fatigue. A specialized in-office assessment determines if Neurolens is right for you.

  • Designed to address eye misalignment behind chronic symptoms
  • Prescribable in any frame style or material
  • Includes a pre-fitting in-office assessment using Neurolens diagnostic technology
  • Most patients report meaningful symptom reduction within weeks
