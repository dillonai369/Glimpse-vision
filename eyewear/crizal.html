Essilor

Crizal

The benchmark anti-reflective coating.

Crizal is Essilor’s premium lens coating line — the most prescribed AR coating in the world. The latest generations (Crizal Sapphire HR, Prevencia, Easy Pro) combine anti-reflective, anti-scratch, anti-smudge, anti-static, and water-repellent properties in a single durable treatment.

  • Anti-reflective coating eliminates glare from screens, headlights, and overhead lighting
  • Crizal Prevencia adds targeted blue-light filtering for digital fatigue
  • Hydrophobic and oleophobic layers stay cleaner, longer
  • Industry-leading scratch and abrasion resistance
