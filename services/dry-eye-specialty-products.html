Macular Health

About AMD — the leading cause
of severe vision loss over 55.

Age-Related Macular Degeneration destroys central vision and affects roughly 15 million people in the U.S. With the aging Baby Boomer population, incidence is expected to triple by 2025. There's no cure — but several risk factors are modifiable, and density of your macular pigment is one of them.

Non-modifiable

Family history. Smoking. Age.

The risk factors you can't change — but they tell us how aggressively we should monitor and supplement.

Modifiable

Low macular pigment.

Light skin/eyes, sun exposure, female sex, obesity, and poor diet all raise risk — and macular pigment density is something we can measure and improve.

In-office

MPOD testing.

Macular Pigment Optical Density — a fast, safe, affordable measurement of your macular pigment thickness. Repeat over time to track improvement.
