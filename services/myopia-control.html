Service

Slow your child's
myopia progression.

Myopia control is a series of treatments and lifestyle habits designed to slow the progression of nearsightedness in children. Studies show specialty lenses and atropine drops can reduce myopia progression by up to 70% over two years. We offer three approaches: orthokeratology (ortho-K) overnight lenses, multifocal soft contacts, and low-dose atropine drops — alone or in combination.

What you get

Myopia Control, in detail.

  • Orthokeratology (ortho-K) — overnight reshaping for daytime freedom
  • Multifocal soft contact lenses designed for myopia control
  • Low-dose atropine eye drops
  • Stellest spectacle lenses (FDA-authorized) — see Lens Treatments
  • Personalized program based on age, progression rate, and lifestyle
  • Regular follow-ups to track progression and adjust treatment
