Lens Care

Good contact lens care
is mostly about habits.

From the initial fitting to ongoing follow-up, we'll teach you how to handle, clean, and store your lenses for years of comfortable, healthy wear.

What you get

Lens Care, in detail.

  • Always wash and dry hands before handling lenses
  • Use only the cleaning solution recommended for your lens type
  • Replace lenses on schedule — daily, bi-weekly, monthly
  • Never sleep in lenses unless they're approved for overnight wear
  • Annual contact lens follow-up to check fit and eye health
  • Replace lens cases every 3 months

Your visit in three steps.

Step 01

Schedule online or by phone.

Whether it's a prescription, a treatment, or a product recommendation — you'll know exactly what's next.
