Journal · Location Guide

Designer eyewear
in Hinsdale, IL.

If you live in the western suburbs of Chicago and you're looking for a real optician — not a chain — you don't need to drive into the city. Glimpse Vision Hinsdale sits at 12 East First Street in downtown Hinsdale, just steps from the Burlington train line, and we've been the destination for independent designer eyewear and integrative eye care in DuPage County since we opened our doors.

Where can I find designer eyeglasses in Hinsdale?

Right here. Our Hinsdale boutique on East First Street carries the full curated collection: Lindberg (Denmark), Chanel, Tom Ford, Dita, Barton Perreira, Anne & Valentin, Thierry Lasry, l.a. Eyeworks, Oliver Goldsmith, Lafont, Theo, Sospiri, Orgreen, and FHone. Every brand on our shelf is independent — family-owned, hand-finished, and selected because nothing else feels like it.

Is there an eye doctor in Hinsdale that does comprehensive eye exams?

Yes — our Hinsdale optometrists offer comprehensive eye exams, pediatric eye exams, and clinical dry eye evaluations. Our exam protocol takes 45-60 minutes and includes retinal imaging, no-puff tonometry, visual field testing, and a real conversation about your screen habits, sleep, and nutrition.

Do you fit children for eyeglasses in Hinsdale?

Yes. We're one of the few western-suburb opticians that fits Stellest spectacle lenses for myopia control (FDA-authorized for slowing nearsightedness in kids by up to 71% over two years). We also fit CooperVision MiSight 1 Day contact lenses, NaturalVue multifocals, and overnight orthokeratology — the full myopia management toolkit.

For families in Hinsdale, Burr Ridge, Western Springs, Oak Brook, Downers Grove, and Clarendon Hills, this means you don't need to drive to a university children's hospital for advanced myopia care. We provide it all in-office.

What brands of designer eyeglasses are available in the western suburbs?

You can find chain-store frames almost anywhere. Independent designer eyewear — the kind that fits well, lasts a decade, and doesn't look like a fashion-house licensing campaign — is harder to find. We carry the houses that matter:

  • Lindberg — Danish, screwless titanium, weighs under 3 grams
  • Chanel — hand-laid Italian acetate, the CC monogram done quietly
  • Tom Ford — made in Italy, sculptural and confident
  • Dita — California-designed, Japanese-executed, jewelry-grade
  • Barton Perreira — vintage Hollywood polish, made in Japan
  • Anne & Valentin — French color theory in eyewear form

Plus 8 more independent houses. We rotate inventory seasonally — the only way to see what's currently in our cases is to walk in.

Can I get a contact lens fitting in Hinsdale?

Yes — we fit the full range of daily disposables (CooperVision MyDay, Clariti 1 Day, Alcon Dailies Total 1), monthlies (Biofinity, Acuvue Oasys), torics for astigmatism, multifocals for presbyopia, and specialty lenses (sclerals, hybrids, RGPs) for irregular corneas and severe dry eye.

We accept VSP, EyeMed, BCBS, MetLife, Cigna, Medicare, and many other vision and medical plans. Call ahead and we'll verify your benefits before your appointment so there are no surprises.

Frequently asked questions.

Who is the best optometrist in Hinsdale, IL?

Our Hinsdale optometrists at Glimpse Vision provide comprehensive eye exams, dry eye evaluations, pediatric exams, and specialty fittings — serving Hinsdale, Western Springs, Burr Ridge, Oak Brook, Clarendon Hills, and the surrounding western suburbs since opening.

Where can I buy Lindberg eyeglasses near Hinsdale, IL?

Glimpse Vision Hinsdale is a certified Lindberg optician serving DuPage County. We do the full Lindberg fitting process in-store: face measurements, color selection, custom build at the Aarhus, Denmark atelier, 3-4 week delivery.

Do you fit kids for myopia control glasses in Hinsdale?

Yes. We fit Stellest (FDA-authorized spectacle lenses), CooperVision MiSight 1 Day contact lenses, NaturalVue multifocals, low-dose atropine, and overnight orthokeratology for children ages 6-15 across the western suburbs.

Is parking available at the Hinsdale store?

Yes — convenient street parking on First Street, plus the Hinsdale Metra commuter parking is two blocks away.

How much is an eye exam in Hinsdale?

With most vision insurance (VSP, EyeMed, BCBS, etc.) your routine eye exam is covered. Without insurance, our comprehensive exam runs $185-$250 depending on retinal imaging and specialty testing performed.

Do you carry Chanel eyewear in the western suburbs?

Yes — we carry the current season of Chanel optical and sunglass collections at our Hinsdale store. We can also source specific Chanel reference numbers from the Italian distributor within a week if not in stock.
