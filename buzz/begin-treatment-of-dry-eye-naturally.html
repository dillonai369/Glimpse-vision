Journal · Eye Health

Begin treatment
of dry eye naturally.

It’s staggering: 30 million Americans have symptomatic dry eyes, but only 16 million are actually diagnosed. Dry eye is considered a multi-factorial condition in both anatomy and physiology — which is why a thorough dry eye evaluation is recommended for anyone with chronic symptoms. But for early or mild cases, there’s a powerful set of natural treatments you can begin at home today.

What drives most chronic dry eye.

Three things, usually in combination:

  1. Meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD). The oil glands along your eyelid margin are clogged or inflamed, so your tear film evaporates too fast.
  2. Systemic inflammation. Diet, gut health, stress, and hormones drive body-wide inflammation that shows up at the eye.
  3. Environmental + behavioral. Screen time, indoor heating, contact lens overwear, mascara residue, dehydration.

The natural starting protocol.

If you’ve been symptomatic for less than 6 months and you don’t have severe pain or vision changes, start here. Most patients see meaningful improvement within 3-4 weeks.

Step 1: Daily lid hygiene.

  • Morning: cleanse the eyelid margin with a tea tree foam cleanser. 30 seconds.
  • After cleansing: spray a hypochlorous acid mist (Heyedrate) over closed eyelids.
  • Evening: remove all eye makeup completely with a non-irritating remover oil.

Step 2: Warm compress 3-4x weekly.

  • Microwave a heat mask 30 seconds.
  • Apply to closed eyelids for 8-10 minutes.
  • Massage gently along the lid margin after the heat.

Step 3: Anti-inflammatory diet.

  • 2-3 servings of fatty fish per week (or omega-3 supplement)
  • Reduce processed foods, sugar, and excess seed oils
  • Add antioxidant-rich foods: kale, spinach, berries, citrus
  • Hydrate: 64+ ounces of water daily

Step 4: Targeted supplementation.

  • EyePromise EZ Tears: 8 anti-inflammatory ingredients including high-dose omega-3. Relief in 1-2 weeks for most patients.
  • Magnesium glycinate: 200-400 mg in the evening if you’re stress-prone.

Step 5: Environment + behavior.

  • Humidifier in the bedroom (40-50%)
  • 20-20-20 screen breaks
  • Switch to daily disposable contact lenses if you wear monthlies
  • Sleep at least 7 hours (tear production is regenerated overnight)
  • Stop sleeping in eye makeup

Step 6: Preservative-free artificial tears, as needed.

For real-time symptom relief use OPTASE Dry Eye Intense Drops. Preservative-free, contact-lens compatible, 300 drops per bottle.

When natural isn’t enough.

If after 6 weeks of consistent natural treatment you’re still symptomatic, that’s the signal that you need a clinical workup. We’ll do tear breakup time, meibography, osmolarity, and lid hygiene assessment — then build a plan that may include prescription drops, Low Light Therapy, or in-office meibomian gland expression.

Frequently asked questions.

How long until natural treatments work?

Most patients see meaningful improvement within 3-4 weeks of consistent daily practice. Full benefit by 8-12 weeks.

Can I just buy artificial tears and skip the rest?

Tears mask symptoms temporarily but don’t fix root cause. You’ll be back at the drugstore in a week. The hygiene + nutrition + behavior changes are what actually shift the disease.

Are these products safe to use indefinitely?

Yes — lid hygiene, omega-3s, warm compresses, and preservative-free drops are all safe for long-term daily use.
